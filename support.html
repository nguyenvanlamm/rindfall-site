How to play

Each level is a cave of cheeses resting on copper sieve plates. Drag a plate along its slot, or tap a zone on the strip under it, to move it to solid, fine (Bites drop through), coarse (Bites and Rounds drop through) or open (everything drops). Fill every crate with the size and count written on it. A cheese in the wrong crate spoils the level; a cheese that rolls out of the cave loses it. Rewind takes back your last slide; Peek shows the next right slide once the cheese has settled.

Purchases

The first cave is free. Deep Cellars is a one-time purchase that opens The Rockers, The Brine and Market Day. The Tasting Board adds 5 Rewinds and 5 Peeks each time you buy it. Prices are shown by the App Store. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.

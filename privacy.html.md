# Rindfall Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-13.

- Stored on the device only: cleared levels, Rewind and Peek counts, the sound setting, Deep Cellars ownership and
  delivered purchases. Deleting the app removes it.
- No account, no advertising, no analytics, crash reporting or tracking; no access to contacts, photos, location,
  camera or microphone.
- Purchases are handled by the App Store; no payment details reach us.
- No personal information is collected from anyone, including children.

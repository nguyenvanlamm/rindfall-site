# Rindfall

A sieve puzzle in a cheese cellar. A physics puzzle for iPhone: slide perforated copper sieves so cheeses drop by size into the right crates.

- 60 levels in 4 caves: The Sieves (free), The Rockers (seesaw flaps), The Brine (Bites float), Market Day (two-size crates).
- No ads, no timers, no lives; plays offline; no data leaves the device.
- Helpers: Rewind (take back the last slide) and Peek (shows the next right slide).
- Purchases: Deep Cellars (one-time, opens caves II–IV); Tasting Board (5 Rewinds + 5 Peeks).
- Status: coming soon to the App Store.

Rindfall

Privacy Policy

Rindfall · last updated 2026-11-13

Rindfall is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the levels you have cleared and how many Rewinds and Peeks you hold.
  • Your settings: whether sound is on.
  • Whether you have bought Deep Cellars, and which purchases have already been delivered.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The first cave, The Sieves, is free. Deep Cellars and the Tasting Board are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
